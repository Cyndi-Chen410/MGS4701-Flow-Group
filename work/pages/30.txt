Program concentrations Program concentrations Specialize in one of three industry-aligned concentrations. Concentration availability varies by campus location. Computer science (Khoury College of Computer Sciences) Learn to build the infrastructure for data-driven products. Cloud computing and distributed systems courses teach you to develop scalable platforms while database classes show you how to organize and query massive datasets. Data, design, and visualization (College of Arts, Media and Design) Learn to turn complex data into compelling, actionable insights through data science, human-centered design, user experience, and critical inquiry — preparing you for in-demand roles combining analytics and storytelling. Engineering theory and modeling (College of Engineering) Focus on applying data techniques to real-world challenges across fields such as renewable energy, chemical processing, and urban infrastructure. Learn to use data for innovations from predicting equipment failure to improving wastewater treatment. Our graduates find success in a wide range of careers, including: Data scientist at Athena Health Data scientist at BNY Mellon Machine learning engineer at Spotify Machine learning engineer at Quantiphi Software engineer at Amazon Web Services Learning outcomes Learning outcomes The MS in Data Science will equip you with technical knowledge and career preparation. As a a graduate, you will have gained proficiency in: Analysis and visualization: Ability to communicate analyses and insights using appropriate and effective visualization techniques that considers the type of data, the application domain, and the audience. Data management: Ability to design, implement, and deploy integrated databases for managing both structured and unstructured large-scale datasets. Machine learning: Ability to select, implement, and evaluate machine learning algorithms, developing an understanding of the limitations of an algorithm for particular application domains. Understand the theoretical and mathematical foundations of both supervised and unsupervised learning techniques. What sets Northeastern’s MS in Data Science program apart?
