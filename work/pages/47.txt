What is a typical business analyst salary? How is business analytics related to digital forensics? Is business intelligence the same as business analytics? * How does the MS in Business Analytics differ from a Master’s in Data Science? Business analytics and data science both deal with structured and unstructured data and aim to provide data-driven solutions. They both heavily use statistical and machine learning models.While these two terms overlap in meaning and are sometimes used exchangeably, they have different emphases. Business analytics often emphasizes providing business insights and storytelling by using statistical, machine learning models, and visualization, which requires communication skills to translate a business problem into the context of data science. Data science has a stronger emphasis on training, testing,and tuning machine learning models in general. * What is a typical business analyst salary? Salary depends upon the industry and position. Starting base salaries are between 75K to 150K. Bonus and stock varies. * How is business analytics related to digital forensics? Digital forensics is one of the important application topics in business analytics. Many digital forensics topics such as detecting bank fraud and social media scams utilize machine learning and statistical models. Business analytics is generic by nature and can apply to many domains including forensic science. * Is business intelligence the same as business analytics? Business intelligence (BI) often refers to the general tools and infrastructure within a company that provides comprehensive metrics of business performances. Many BI tools utilize business analytics models and techniques. Business analytics is more recent and more inclusive in terms of methodology (machine learning, statistics, and visualization) and uses both internal and external company data (such as social media data).
