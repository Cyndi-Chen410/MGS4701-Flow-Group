Online MS in Business Analytics applicants may also [submit a FastApp](https://online.northeastern.edu/analytics/fast-app/). ![Image 9](https://damore-mckim.northeastern.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/data-analytics-research-lab1280x720px-1024x576.jpg) ## Turn data into business impact * Build in-demand skills in Python, R, SQL, Tableau, predictive modeling, machine learning, AI-driven decision-making, and tools like these. * Bridge analytics and strategy to turn data into actionable insights that drive organizational impact. * Apply your skills to real business challenges through your [capstone project](https://damore-mckim.northeastern.edu/capstone-project/) and optional experiences like [graduate co-op](https://damore-mckim.northeastern.edu/graduate-career-center/students/experience-fueled-learning/graduate-co-op/) or the [Community Impact Initiative](https://damore-mckim.northeastern.edu/graduate-career-center/students/experience-fueled-learning/community-impact-initiatives/). * Customize your learning path by choosing between two campus locations or online study, and a range of electives. ## Program options ### Become a data-driven leader through transformative experiences Our MS in Business Analytics prepares the next generation of leaders for our data-driven, technology-first world. In this experience-powered, STEM-designated program, you'll learn from world-class faculty as you build essential skills in quantitative analysis techniques, visualization, and information management. When you graduate, you'll have the knowledge and experience you need to tackle real-world business challenges and shape strategic decisions. Choose your format, location, and pace of study You can pursue the MS in Business Analytics on your terms, choosing the full-time, on-ground option in Boston or Oakland—or a fully online format designed for working professionals. On-campus study immerses you in a collaborative environment with direct access to faculty, peers, and networking opportunities, and international students may qualify for F-1 visa work authorization after graduation. The online option, which typically takes 20 months to complete, delivers the same rigorous, STEM-designated curriculum. Thanks to the flexible pacing, you can balance your studies with career and personal commitments from anywhere in the world. #### Typical full-time program timeline ![Image 10](https://damore-mckim.northeastern.edu/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/DMSB_CurriculumGraphic_FTMSQF_Desktop.png)![Image 11](https://damore-mckim.northeastern.edu/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/DMSB_CurriculumGraphic_FTMSBA_Mobile-580x1024.png) [Explore the full-time curriculum map](https://damore-mckim.northeastern.edu/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/FT_MS_Business_Analytics_CurriculumMap.pdf) Complete required coursework and choose electives In your core courses, you'll learn to interpret, visualize, and leverage data to make strategic decisions and drive business growth. You'll also choose nine credits of electives to build your expertise in areas that interest you as you pursue your unique career goals. _Please note: You need to complete an undergraduate, full-semester course in statistics before you enroll in the MS in Business Analytics._ [Explore Boston requirements](https://catalog.northeastern.edu/graduate/business/master-science/business-analytics-ms/) [Explore Oakland requirements](https://catalog.northeastern.edu/graduate/business/master-science/business-analytics-ms-oak/) [Explore online requirements](https://catalog.northeastern.edu/graduate/business/master-science/business-analytics-online-ms/) Fuel your learning with experience At D'Amore-McKim, we excel at blending classroom instruction and academic theory with experience-powered learning. This approach broadens your knowledge, fosters creativity, and equips you to navigate the evolving demands of today's business world. From the moment you begin your program to the day you graduate, you'll gain valuable experiences that enhance your perspective and boost your confidence. * **Learn** from renowned faculty who are seasoned practitioners and thought leaders * **Study** real-world cases that vividly illustrate course concepts * **Apply** theories in the workplace for immediate, practical impact * FOR Credit ### Required Capstone Project Apply your business analytics expertise to collect, visualize, analyze, and manage data from a real company to address a challenge or question. Yes, the MS in Business Analytics is a STEM-designated program. Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM) designated degrees offer international students on F-1 visas the opportunity to apply for extended Optional Practical Training (OPT). [Explore working after graduation](https://international.northeastern.edu/ogs/employment/working-after-graduation/24-stem-opt/) Is an MS in Business Analytics worth it? Yes, the MS in Business Analytics at Northeastern University's D'Amore-McKim School of Business is definitely worth it for students interested in jobs like business analyst or data scientist or working professionals aiming to lead data-driven strategy in their organizations. This program will help you build in-demand skills in Python, R, SQL, Tableau, and AI, and then apply your skills to real business challenges through your capstone project and optional experiences like graduate co-op or the Community Impact Initiative. How do I apply to the MS in Business Analytics and what will I need? To apply to the MS in Business Analytics, log in or create an account on our[application portal](https://enroll.northeastern.edu/portal/apply). You will need: * Application form * A personal statement (<=500 words) * Resumé * Two letters of recommendation * Academic transcript(s) from all undergraduate and graduate schools attended * Optional GMAT/GRE test scores * TOEFL, IELTS, Duolingo, or PTE Academic (if applicable) * Passport or permanent residency card (if applicable) [Learn more about applying to D'Amore-McKim](https://damore-mckim.northeastern.edu/admissions/how-to-apply/) Is the GMAT or GRE required for admission? Northeastern University's D'Amore-McKim School of Business is currently test-optional and does not require applicants to submit GRE or GMAT scores. Are there any prerequisite courses I need to take before I enroll in the MS in Business Analytics? The MS in Business Analytics program requires applicants to have taken an undergraduate-level, full-semester course in Statistics. What type of jobs can you get with a Master's in Business Analytics? The MS in Business Analytics degree prepares you for careers across various industries and sectors with positions in management, operations, analytics, and more. You'll be ready to pursue roles such as data scientists, analysts, business intelligence engineers, and analytics managers. Explore the wide range of career paths a master's in business analytics can unlock. [Learn more about analytics careers](https://damore-mckim.northeastern.edu/resources/cc-what-type-of-jobs-can-you-get-with-a-masters-in-business-analytics/) Is the program accredited? Yes, the MS in Business Analytics program is accredited. The D'Amore-McKim School of Business is accredited by the highest standard of achievement for business schools worldwide, the Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business International. How long does the MS in Business Analytics program take to complete? The MS in Business Analytics program is full-time and typically completed in 12 or 16 months. Alternatively, the MS in Business Analytics is also offered in a 20 month online format. [Learn more about the online program](https://online.northeastern.edu/business/business-analytics/) Do I need a business degree to earn an MS in Business Analytics? You do not need a business degree to earn a master's degree. Non-business majors are encouraged to apply to our MS in Business Analytics program. We welcome students from all academic and professional backgrounds. How much does the MS in Business Analytics cost? All tuition and fees are determined annually and subject to revision by Northeastern University, which reserves the right to alter the schedule of tuition and fees without notice.
