Do I need to submit original hard copy at application stage? A. You are not required to mail the original documents at this stage. Please make sure that you have uploaded all supporting documents in the application system for review. Applicants will be contacted by our programme office for additional information or calcification on documents, when needed. Q. When will I get the result after submitting the application? A. The applications will be reviewed after the deadline of each round. Usually the shortlisted candidate will receive offer/ conditional offer one and a half month after each deadline. Q. I submitted the application in the first round but the result is still pending after few months. Do I have chance to be admitted? A. Application offers are released on a rolling basis until all places have been filled. Even if you are not shortlisted in the first round, your application will be considered in subsequent rounds if quota is available. Q. Is there any interview? A. No interview is required for our programmes. Q. Do I need to submit Graduate Record Examinations ( GRE) ? A. GRE is not required by our programme. Question Not here?
