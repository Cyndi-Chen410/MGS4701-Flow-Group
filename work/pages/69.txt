Press escape key to return to main menu Students Staff Alumni King's College London You are viewing a course in < Computer science Postgraduate degree Overview The Data Science MSc provides advanced technical and practical skills in the collection, collation, curation and analysis of data. This is an ideal study pathway for graduates with a background in quantitative subjects, or who possess relevant work experience, who want to gain experience in current methods and techniques of data science. The course covers computational and statistical methods, analysis, and key skills in programming, data visualisation and presentation. You will also explore approaches to harnessing emerging AI technologies. Key benefits 6th in the UK for Computer Science (QS World Rankings 2026). The Department of Informatics has a reputation for delivering research-led teaching and project supervision from leading experts in their field. You'll interact with world-class experts in many exciting areas of Computer Science, including Algorithms and Data Analysis, Cybersecurity, Human-Centred Computing and Software Systems. You'll study a wide-range of innovative modules, covering both the theory and practice of modern Computer Science. Friendly and supportive learning environment, with students from across the globe. Course essentials Our Data Science MSc explores current and emerging approaches to managing, analysing and interpreting data across a wide range of applications. You will develop an understanding of both the potential and limitations of different techniques, building the knowledge and practical skills needed to tackle real-world data challenges. You will study key areas including statistics, data mining, databases, data warehousing and information retrieval, while developing practical programming skills in Python and SQL. You can tailor the course to your interests and career ambitions through a range of optional modules, including opportunities to study modules offered by the Department of Mathematics. You can explore areas such as statistics in finance, statistical learning, machine learning, neural networks and deep learning, data visualisation, and communicating insights through data. As your degree progresses, you will undertake a major project that brings together the knowledge and skills you have developed. You will create an original artefact, such as a piece of software, code or prototype, in response to a research or industry challenge. Through researching, designing, building, testing and refining your solution, you will develop valuable skills in project management, design and evaluation. You may also have the opportunity to gain valuable industry experience by working with a company on your project. Working as part of a team with students from different backgrounds, you could develop a software solution for an external partner in a sector such as computing, law, aviation or government. The course is taught across King’s College London’s Strand and Waterloo campuses , placing you at the heart of London and within easy reach of the city’s academic, cultural and social opportunities. The Department of Informatics is based in Bush House on the Strand Campus.
