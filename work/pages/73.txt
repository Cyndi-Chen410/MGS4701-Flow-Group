Course delivery Module information Teaching staff Entry requirements Fees and scholarships Application process Career opportunities Related courses Upcoming events Student experience Apply now Postgraduate Open Day: Saturday 14 November Course overview Ranked 68th globally (QS, 2027), Birmingham's MSc Business Analytics teaches you to turn data into decisions. You’ll analyse real datasets and turn your findings into actionable recommendations for organisations, from start-ups to global companies. You'll start by building foundational skills in business analytics. Business Analytics and the Digital Organisation introduces how data functions inside modern organisations, while Data Analytics and Predictive Modelling develops the statistical and modelling skills you'll use throughout the course. You'll apply these skills to specific business functions, including: Applied Analytics for Operations, Supply Chain and Logistics Marketing Analytics and Behavioural Science Machine Learning and Data Analytics in Finance You will gain hands-on experience with industry software including python and tableau. Alongside this, Analytics Micro Courses let you build additional specialist knowledge in areas that match your own interests and career plans. On the Business Analytics Capstone Project, you'll apply everything you've learned to a real dataset supplied by an industry partner or a University of Birmingham department. You'll take the project through the full analytics cycle, framing the problem, analysing the data, and presenting recommendations. Beyond your studies, you can join a residential trip to the Raymond Priestley Centre , the University's outdoor education centre on Coniston Water in the Lake District. The residential trip is built around teamwork, communication and problem-solving exercises. Sign up now Why study Business Analytics at Birmingham? Birmingham Business School's strategic focus is on architecting change for a responsible future, and analytics sits at the centre of that. As organisations face growing pressure to make decisions that are both commercially sound and responsible, the ability to interpret data accurately and use it responsibly is becoming essential to making better decisions and creating meaningful impact. You'll have access to the University's Bloomberg training suite , giving you hands-on experience with the financial data and analytics tools used across the finance and business industry. Through City-REDI , the University's regional economic development institute, you'll also see how data and analytics research is applied to real policy and economic questions facing the West Midlands and beyond, connecting classroom learning to live regional impact. The Centre for Responsible Business brings a further dimension to your studies, exploring how organisations can use data and analytics responsibly, considering not just what a business can do with data, but what it should do. You may also have the opportunity to apply for a B-Experienced internship , gaining practical workplace experience with a regional or national employer alongside your studies. Studying in Birmingham, the UK's largest business hub outside London , means these connections are close at hand, giving you access to a wide network of employers, alumni, and industry partners throughout your degree. Organisations across every sector need people who can turn raw data into decisions that matter. What you'll be equipped for Studying business analytics gives you the technical and strategic skillset to translate complex datasets into business insight. You'll graduate ready to step into roles such as: Business Analyst - bridging data teams and decision-makers, identifying opportunities for growth and efficiency Data Analyst / Data Scientist - building models and dashboards that shape strategy Management Consultant - advising organisations on data-led transformation Marketing Analyst - optimising campaigns and customer insight through data Supply Chain Analyst - using predictive analytics to streamline operations Financial Analyst - applying quantitative methods to investment and risk decisions Beyond the job title A business analytics qualification doesn't just open doors to "analyst" roles. It builds a transferable skillset - statistical reasoning, data visualisation, commercial judgement - that underpins careers in strategy, operations, product management, and general management, giving graduates flexibility.
