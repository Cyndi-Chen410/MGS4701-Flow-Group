Admissions We welcome students with a wide range of interests and aspirations. Applicants are reviewed as a whole person. The selection committee will consider all parts of every application submitted, including the essays and recommendations. We want the best students in our master's program, but we do not have a maximum or minimum number of students that we accept each year. Meet with our Admissions Coach Image Our Admissions Coach can help you with your questions about admissions requirements, filling out your application, degree options, how to qualify for merit scholarships, completing your next steps to enroll, and all you have to look forward to as an Eller Wildcat. Reserve an Appointment via the Calendly Link Below Schedule 1:1 Application Deadline Summer Start Only October 19: Round 1 December 14: Round 2 February 1: Round 3 + International Student Deadline March 31: Round 4 June 14: Domestic Student Deadline Note: all applications received before the specified deadlines above will be reviewed and students will receive an admittance decision before the next deadline. For those submitting between Feb 1st and May 31st, those applications will be reviewed on a rolling basis. * Contact the program manager, Michelle Piontek , for possible extensions to the int'l deadline Apply Now Admissions Requirements Applicants may be asked to participate in a short (15 to 25 minute) virtual interview to discuss their interests in the program and to assess their quantitative background and preparedness for the MSBA program. This requirement replaces the need to complete and submit scores for the GRE or GMAT. A member of the admissions team will reach out to schedule this meeting if you meet the basic criteria for the program. An undergraduate degree from a regionally accredited college or university (domestic students) or a U.S.-comparable bachelor degree (international students). An undergraduate GPA of 3.00 or higher. International applicants must calculate their entrance GPA using the UA Graduate College GPA Calculator . PLUS applicants will be asked to upload a screen shot of the GPA results during the application process. For University of Arizona international undergraduates looking to continue their education in an Eller Graduate program—June 1 is your final application deadline. When applying, you will also need to provide the following: School Codes TOEFL: 4832 (The University of Arizona) Test Scores For international applicants only Copies of TOEFL/IELTS scores ( uploaded during online application process ) For TOEFL tests taken prior to January 21, 2026: Minimum score of 85 iBT or Home Edition iBT. For tests taken on or after January 21, 2026: Minimum overall score of 4.5 on the TOEFL 1-6 scale. IELTS test composite score of 7 (no subject area below 6 ) Transcripts Copies of official transcripts/academic records ( uploaded during online application process ) Official transcripts/academic records (official documents needed ONLY after being admitted into program.) From a US institution: Final, official transcript showing degree awarded with conferred date From an international institution: Final, official transcripts/academic records showing degree awarded with conferred date and/or official degree/diploma certificate More details here on accepted transcripts/academic records For final admission, you may be required to provide an official transcript and diploma in both your native language and the English translation. Resume In addition to work experience with dates of employment, your resume should include education, awards and honors, memberships, leadership experience, and participation in professional organizations. ( uploaded during online application process ) Statement of Purpose We welcome applications from a wide range of academic backgrounds, professional interests, and personal aspirations. As a way of sharing your personal vision, please prepare a written statement that describes why you are interested in a career in information systems, your specific interests in the academic programs of the Eller College of Management, and how earning a Master of Science in Business Analytics from the University of Arizona will contribute to you achieving your professional goals. (uploaded during online application process) Recommendations Requests from two to three professional associates who can speak to your eligibility for this program. You will identify these individuals within the application, and they will be asked to complete a short form regarding your suitability for a graduate program in Business Analytics.
