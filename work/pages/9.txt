Learn more Real-world experience Gaining real-world experience in your graduate program is essential. That’s why all master's in business analytics students are required to take an experiential learning course during their program. Through our Center for Experiential Learning or the Center for Analytics and Business Insights , you’ll gain valuable, hands-on experience and build meaningful relationships with decision-makers near and far. Whether you work with a Fortune 500 company, startup, or nonprofit, you build your resume and enhance your leadership and problem-solving skills.
