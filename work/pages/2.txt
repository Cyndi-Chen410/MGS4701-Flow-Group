Developed through extensive collaboration with industry leaders, alumni, and students, the program bridges advanced quantitative analysis with strategic business insights. The curriculum emphasizes data-driven decision-making, utilizing statistical modeling, optimization, and predictive analytics to resolve complex operational challenges and improve business performance. Students receive rigorous exposure to optimization and stochastic modeling, along with in-depth coverage of practical analytics applications. As data becomes central to global infrastructure, this program equips graduates to lead innovation across business, healthcare, government, and numerous other sectors of the economy. Graduate studies in Business Analytics consist of 36 credits, assuming adequate student preparation in probability and statistics. To graduate, students must earn a minimum of 18 credits within IEOR and a minimum of 15 credits from Columbia Business School. With department approval, additional elective credits can be taken from Columbia Business School or Columbia’s other graduate schools, including the School of International and Public Affairs or the Graduate School of Arts and Sciences. Additional details are available on the MSBA website at msba.engineering.columbia.edu/content/curriculum . Required Core Courses (18 Credits) NOTE: To satisfy the degree requirements, students must complete 12 credits of core courses within IEOR and 6 credits within Columbia Business School (CBS).
