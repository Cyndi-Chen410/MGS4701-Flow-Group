Two Specialized Paths to Success. One Rigorous Chicago Booth Foundation. Booth’s specialized masters programs—the Master in Management Program and the Asness and Liew Master in Finance Program —are designed for recent graduates who are ready to turn their academic strengths into professional impact. Through a rigorous, transformative curriculum taught by world-class faculty, students build business fluency, analytical depth, and career momentum that starts immediately and grows throughout their careers. What Is a Specialized Masters Program? A specialized masters program is a graduate-level degree focused on a specific field or industry. They provide in-depth expertise and practical skills tailored to a particular profession, helping students advance or switch careers in their chosen domain. In the specialized masters programs at Chicago Booth, high-achieving recent college graduates build on their academic experience at one of the world’s most respected business schools, taught by world-class faculty whose ideas shape today’s global business landscape. Tap Your Career Potential with a World-Class Business Education A Booth degree opens doors around the globe. Graduates of Booth’s business masters programs launch their careers across consulting, finance, technology, analytics, strategy, and business operations. Employers value Booth graduates not just for what they know, but for how they think—analytically, independently, and with confidence and clarity in complex situations. Students develop capabilities that support: Enhanced early career opportunities Faster progression into roles with greater responsibility Adaptability as industries, technologies, and careers evolve This is an education designed not only to launch your early career, but to support sustained success over your entire professional journey. Specialized masters students experience Booth as a cohort, taking their educational journey together as a tight-knit peer group. Our specialized masters programs share a common Booth foundation: Rigorous core coursework combined with intensive specialized classes Faculty at the forefront of research and practice An academic environment grounded in real-world relevance Master in Management Master in Finance Designed for recent college graduates who: Are high-achieving and seeking to learn the fundamentals of business. Studied the liberal arts, STEM, or business-related fields in college and want to create a successful career in business. Want to build broad business and analytical skills and learn how they can apply them to solve complex business problems. Designed for recent college graduates who: Are from a quantitative field of study and want to build the rigorous financial foundation needed to succeed in demanding, data-driven roles. Want deep, technical, and analytical training in finance so they can evaluate investments, manage risk, and make informed financial decisions. Are preparing for careers in finance-focused roles and want the skills and confidence to launch and accelerate careers in investment banking, asset management, private equity, fintech, corporate finance, or other related fields. Time to Complete: 10 months Time to Complete: 15 months Program Kickoff: Both programs begin with the Boothcamp experience in August, a two-week experience full of academic, career, and professional development training. Boothcamp has a rigorous academic schedule taught by Booth professors Lara Kattan and Kathleen Fitzgerald. While they are not credit courses, they are an important part of Boothcamp. An Academic Experience That Evolves with the World Learn how to think, not what to think, about business. At Booth, the curriculum is never static. Faculty bring cutting-edge research, real-world application, and industry insight directly into the classroom, creating an academic experience that evolves as the world changes, alongside markets, organizations, and technology. Students develop a number of skills, including: Strong judgment Analytical rigor Problem-solving skills These strengths extend far beyond any single role or industry—preparing students to navigate complexity throughout their careers.
