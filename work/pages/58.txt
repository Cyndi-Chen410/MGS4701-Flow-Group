Application Portal MS in Business Analytics & AI Admission Requirements The MSBAi application process is straightforward. Here's everything you'll need to submit a complete application. 1. Application To apply to the MS in Business Analytics & AI program, use Lehigh's online application system . There is an application fee of $50. 2. Essay As part of the application process, we require all applicants to submit an essay. This essay is an essential component of the evaluation process and provides us with valuable insights into your thoughts, experiences, and motivations. Please find the details below. Essay Guidelines: Length: 1 to 2 pages Format: PDF or Word document Submission: Please upload the document in the designated section of the application portal The essay prompt will be provided within the application portal, and we encourage you to take the time to reflect on the topic and articulate your thoughts effectively. 3. Resume or CV Your professional resume or CV should list your education and professional achievements. Please include your computing skills. The resume or CV should be uploaded in the online application. 4. GMAT or GRE Test Score (optional) The GMAT or GRE test score submission is Optional* for MS in Business Analytics & AI (MSBAi) applicants who have earned an undergraduate degree from an accredited college or university. Applicants who choose not to submit a GMAT or GRE test score should provide us with evidence in their application that they can be successful in the MSBAi program without a GMAT or GRE score. Applications will be reviewed by the admissions committee, but this is no guarantee of acceptance. Please see a list of examples below that would be considered as evidence of the ability to be successful in the program without a GMAT or GRE exam: An overall undergraduate GPA of 3.3 or better A grade of B or better in two (2) or more quantitative courses Applicants choosing not to submit test scores will receive one of three responses from the admissions committee upon completion of all other application materials: Acceptance into the MSBAi program Request from the Admissions Committee for a GMAT or GRE test score submission Denied Admission into the MSBAi Program * “OPTIONAL” means it is at the discretion of the applicant whether they feel a GMAT or GRE test score submission strengthens their application. GMAT scores will be accepted for evaluation as long as your official score is available through the testing service. Please indicate on your online application the date you are taking [or have taken] the GMAT. GMAT test center, test information and test preparation materials can be found by visiting the Graduate Management Admission Council ® [GMAC] website at www.mba.com . Please use the proper program code shown below to have your official scores sent to Lehigh. Our office keeps GMAT scores on file for a period of five years. Official scores normally arrive at the Graduate Programs Office two weeks after the exam has been taken. GMAT Program Code: 6ZB-FN-07 You may find a GRE test center, test information and preparation materials by visiting the Educational Testing Service ® (ETS) website. www.ets.org/gre/ . Lehigh’s GRE institution code number is 2365. The department code is 4201. 5. Transcripts Applying with Unofficial Transcripts Unofficial transcripts are accepted when submitting an application. Unofficial transcripts are also acceptable for application review. Domestic and international applicants should upload unofficial transcripts in English that show the degree received, along with course grades and cumulative grades. International applicants must hold a bachelor's degree from a U.S. institution, or the equivalent of a bachelor's degree from a non-U.S. institution of higher education. Submitting Official Transcripts If admitted into the program, you will be required to submit official transcripts from all universities attended, and all post-secondary education including transfer credits. Official final transcripts must bear the signature of the university’s registrar, the seal of the issuing institution, and contain the name and date of the degree awarded. Below are the accepted processes when submitting official transcripts. From a U.S. or Canadian Higher Education Institution Official electronic transcripts via an official secure transcript service are the preferred method of receipt. If the institution offers an electronic secure transcript service, please have the institution submit documentation to the following email address: business@lehigh.edu . If the institution does not offer an official electronic transcript service, the official transcripts and degree certificates must be mailed in a sealed envelope with the institution seal and sent directly to our office using the address listed below. From a non-U.S. Higher Education Institution Official transcripts and degree certificates via mail is the preferred method of receipt. All documents must be in a sealed envelope with the institution’s seal and sent directly to our office. Hand-delivered transcripts will only be accepted if they are submitted in the original sealed envelope issued by the institution. Transcripts can be mailed or hand-delivered to the address below. Once complete, a web link will automatically be emailed to the recommender. Letters from family members or friends will not be considered. Paper copies of letters of recommendation will not be accepted. 7. Admissions Interview An interview with a member of our admissions committee may be requested. Selection for an interview is dependent on applicant background and does not increase your chances of admission. 8. English Proficiency Lehigh Business Graduate Programs provides multiple pathways for applicants to demonstrate English language proficiency. Learn more about the various English proficiency pathways. Enrollment Deposit All students must submit a $1,000 tuition enrollment deposit when accepting an offer of admission . The deposit secures your place in the program and will be applied toward tuition. The deposit must be received by the deadline noted in your offer letter. To apply for a Lehigh graduate program, or access an in-progress application, use Lehigh's online application system . Application fees may apply. What is the application deadline for Lehigh University’s MSBAi program? The Master's in Business Analytics & AI program accepts applications on a rolling bases, with the final application deadline on June 1. We recommend gathering your materials at least a few months in advance to allow time for transcripts, letters of recommendation, and any supporting documents. Can I visit Lehigh’s campus? The Graduate Programs Office staff is happy to arrange a campus visit for you. While at Lehigh, you may opt to meet with an admissions counselor, tour the college or university, meet current students, visit a class, or schedule a formal interview. Please contact the Graduate Programs Office at business@lehigh.edu or by phone at (610) 758-4450. Not near campus? Explore Lehigh’s virtual tour! Do I need to take the GMAT or GRE to apply to a business master's program? No. The MSBAi is a full-time, on-campus program with no GMAT or GRE requirement—test scores are optional. Applicants who choose not to submit a GMAT or GRE score should provide evidence of academic readiness. Do I need to submit official transcripts when applying? No. Unofficial transcripts are accepted for application review. Domestic and international applicants should upload unofficial English-language transcripts showing the degree received, along with course and cumulative grades. You will only be required to submit official transcripts if admitted to the program. Learn more about submitting official transcripts after admission. When can I start the MS in Business Analytics & AI program? The MSBAi program starts every year during the Fall. Admission is rolling, so you can apply at any point during the year. Are there any MSBAi prerequisites? Lehigh’s MS in Business Analytics & AI welcomes students from a wide range of academic and professional backgrounds, with no formal prerequisite courses required for admission. Pre-Master's Bootcamp Your MSBAi experience begins with BUAN 450: Foundations of Business Analytics and AI, a required two-week bootcamp before the fall semester. Covering statistics, data analysis, programming, and data visualization, the course builds a foundation for the graduate coursework ahead. Whether you’re new to these subjects or looking to refresh your skills, the bootcamp helps prepare you for success in the program. Prior experience in these areas—through coursework, professional experience, or independent learning—can be beneficial, but isn’t required. To get a head start, you may also explore optional online resources, such as Coursera. Have questions about your background or how to prepare? Contact business@lehigh.edu. We’re happy to help. Can I take MSBAi courses before being admitted to the degree program? Through our accelerated 4+1 programs, Lehigh undergraduate students with available space in their schedules can take and reserve up to 12 graduate-level (or equivalent) courses during their undergraduate studies, helping them finish the master's degree and join the workforce within one year! Learn more about the acclereated 4+1 MSBAi program. Is an interview required for MS in Business Analytics & AI admission? While not required, interviews are offered at the request of the admissions committee, typically to clarify or further explore an aspect of the application. What factors do the admissions committee consider when evaluating my application? Each applicant presents a unique skill set for consideration. Your application is carefully evaluated on your academic abilities, motivation, and maturity to complete graduate studies successfully. Evaluated areas also include past academic performance, leadership skills, accomplishments, and contribution to the learning environment. How can I gauge my chances for admission to the Lehigh Business MSBAi program? The Graduate Programs Office is happy to schedule an individual appointment with you to discuss your credentials, your goals and the opportunities available to you to increase your chances of admission. To schedule an appointment, please contact us at business@lehigh.edu . Does Lehigh accept transfer credits to the Lehigh MSBAi program? No. Lehigh does not accept transfer credits to the MS in Business Analytics & AI program. When will I be notified of acceptance to the program? Generally, 2-4 weeks after your completed application has been submitted. Can I change my program pace within the master's in business analtyics & AI program? Students usually complete the full-time MSBAi program within 10 months. A 16-month program extension is available to students who choose to complete a summer internship. The decision to extend the degree must be made by the start of the spring semester.
