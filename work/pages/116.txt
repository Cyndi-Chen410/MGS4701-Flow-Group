Therefore, early applications are strongly encouraged. The admission exercise for our MSc programmes 2027-28 will start from September 1, 2026 to March 16, 2027. You may visit https://www.se.cuhk.edu.hk/programmes/postgraduate-programmes/ to know more about the course details Online Applicaiton: Submit application through Online Application System . Pay the non-refundable application fee of HK$500. Upload the scanned copy of transcript and other supporting documents to the Online Application System for review. Shortlisted applicants will be asked to mail the hardcopy of supporting documents to us. All documents submitted will not be returned. Applicants are required to provide true, accurate and complete information in support of the applications. Otherwise, the applications will not be process.
