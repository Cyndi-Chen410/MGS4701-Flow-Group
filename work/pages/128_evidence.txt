Apply now Apply now LOCATION Singapore University of Technology and Design (SUTD) PROGRAMME MODE & CANDIDATURE Full-time (12 months) Part-time (24 months, maximum 36 months with special approval) GRADUATE WITH Master of Science in Technology and Design (Data Science) Overview Programme schedule Application Requirement to graduate Financing Fees Billing and payment modes Application timeline Applications for the September 2027 intake are now open until 31 May 2027. Applications are reviewed in batches and applicants are encouraged to apply early. Criteria for admission There is one intake for the MTD (Data Science) programme each year in September. Applicants should possess the following: At least a bachelor’s degree in a related discipline including Engineering, Mathematics, Economics, Statistics, Computer Science, and other Applied Sciences; Students are expected to have knowledge of basic calculus, linear algebra, probability and statistics. Familiarity with a programming language (Python or R) will be beneficial; Students whose language of instruction during undergraduate study is not English are required to present TOEFL-iBT (min. 85) or IELTS (min. 6.0) score report; Interested candidates with undergraduate degrees in an unrelated discipline may be considered on a case-by-case basis*; Related job experiences will be considered for admission. Note: TOEFL / IELTS scores are valid for two years from the test date and should not have expired at the point of application. Expired scores will not be considered for the application. * Applicants with a bachelor’s degree not relevant to the programme can submit a resumé or portfolio highlighting your skills, abilities and experiences to augment your application. All applications are considered holistically by the MTD Admissions Committee. Decision by the MTD Admissions Committee will be final. Meeting the minimum admission requirements does not guarantee admission. Documents to submit Before starting your online application, please prepare the following documents (in PDF format) / information: NRIC (front and back) for Singapore Citizens/Permanent Residents, or Passport (info page) for international applicants Bachelor’s degree and academic transcript Acceptable English Proficiency score report (if applicable) Names, email addresses and institutions/organisations of two referees Statement of Objectives / Personal Statement (not less than 500 words) Curriculum Vitae (CV) Applications must be accompanied by all required supporting documents to be reviewed. The online application takes approximately 10 to 15 minutes to complete. Documents not issued in English should be accompanied by an official or certified English translation. Application fees All applicants are required to pay a non-refundable application fee of S$21.80 (inclusive of prevailing Goods & Services Tax). Application process All applications must be submitted via the SUTD Admissions System . Check your eligibility Review the admission requirements for your chosen MTD Programme. Prepare your documents Prepare all required supporting documents before starting your application. Submit your online application Complete the online application and pay the application fee. Application review Applications will be reviewed in batches. Interview, where applicable Depending on the assessment of the application, applicants may receive a direct offer or be shortlisted for an interview. Application outcome Applicants will be informed of the outcomes by email and/or through the application portal. Application enquiries For enquiries, please email to MTD@sutd.edu.sg . For applicants residing in China, please email to MTD_CN@sutd.edu.sg .
