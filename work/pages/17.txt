GRE Institution Code: 1996 GMAT Institution Code: 9DD-X7-77 Global Candidates Applicants must have a fluent command of rapid, idiomatic English; they must be able to express thoughts clearly in speech and in writing. Applicants who have not completed a degree within the United States or who have not earned a university degree in an English-speaking country (not simply an English-speaking institution), or who have not lived and worked full-time in an English-speaking country for the past four years must submit scores from the Test of English as a Foreign Language (TOEFL) , or the International English Language Testing System (IELTS) . We expect international applicants to obtain a score of 100 or higher (internet-based), 250 (computer-based), or 600 (paper-based) on the TOEFL and a 7 on the IELTS. The TOEFL and IELTS scores are valid for two years. We consider the TOEFL or IELTS score along with the GRE/GMAT scores in the verbal and AWA sections, essays, and the interview to determine a candidate's language readiness to compete in and contribute to the William & Mary MSBA community. Our Institution Code for TOEFL is B308. Please have the scores sent to the office of MSBA Admissions. Please submit official test score reports. We do not accept copies. If you have pursued an undergraduate degree in the U.S. or another English-speaking country, then you may be eligible for a waiver for the language-proficiency test. We will waive the test requirement in the following circumstances: You have studied for at least four years in an English-speaking country. You completed your undergraduate degree during that time. Upload Transcripts To expedite the application review process, applicants must submit an unofficial transcript. Note: unofficial transcripts will not appear as "complete" on the checklist until a candidate has submitted official reports at the time of enrollment. Please disregard this checklist status. Once admitted, you will be required to provide an official transcript from your degree-granting institution. Students are unable to begin classes without an official transcript. Official transcripts should arrive in a sealed envelope with the signature of the Registrar across the seal. Do not open official transcripts. Official transcripts can also be submitted electronically to [[m|masontranscripts]] via your institution's electronic vendor and using secure portals. A transcript sent directly by the applicant will not be accepted as official. William & Mary graduates are only required to upload an unofficial transcript. Transcripts for study abroad programs or transfer credits are not required if the course and grade are included on the transcript of the degree-granting institution but can be requested by the Admissions Committee if deemed necessary. If your transcripts are in other languages other than English, also submit translated versions. Upon receipt of the official transcript, the status will be updated in the application checklist. We encourage you to conduct the interview in person in conjunction with a campus visit, but we understand that this is not possible for all candidates. For applicants unable to meet in person, interviews are also offered virtually. Prerequisites & Summer Prep A distinguished strength of the William & Mary MSBA program is that 1/3 of our curriculum teaches advanced skills in Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence, preparing students to design, implement, and interpret cutting-edge analytical models. We have structured the MSBA curriculum so that students begin coursework at the intermediate level. To ensure you are fully prepared for the Master of Science in Business Analytics program, all admitted students must complete the following prerequisites and attend the MSBA Required Bootcamp before beginning the program: Topics Expand All Expand Required Prerequisite: Statistics This prerequisite can be met by the following: W&M BUAD 231, BUAD 512A, MATH 106, MATH 351, ECON 307, PSYC 301, SOCL 353, KINE 394 BUAD 231 is offered as a residential summer course from June 8 - 19, 2026 BUAD 512A is offered as an online MSBA prerequisite course from May 8 - June 24, 2026 Virginia Community College System - MTH 240 If you plan to take or have taken an alternative college level course in Statistics within the last 5 years, please submit the class syllabus for curriculum approval before enrolling. If a student has taken AP Statistics and/or Probability, an additional higher-level college-level course must be completed. Introduction to Corporate Finance Introduction to Financial Accounting Introduction to Marketing Introduction to Operations Management Required Bootcamp: Business Analytics Toolkit All admitted students are automatically registered for the Business Analytics Toolkit Bootcamp, a comprehensive two-week program that consolidates prerequisite material and is held between our MSBA Orientation. The combined cost for orientation and bootcamp is $1,350, and both are required components of the MSBA program taught by William & Mary faculty. The Business Analytics Toolkit Bootcamp is a required, in-person experience that begins in August and provides an intensive introduction to the programming and mathematical foundations needed for success in the MSBA program. Students build skills in R, Python, and core quantitative methods through hands-on practice with data, coding exercises, and applied problem solving. By the end of the bootcamp, students are prepared to begin MSBA coursework at the intermediate level with confidence. Your final grade is made up of three components: participation (10%), in- and out-of-class assessments (20%), and the final exam (70%). To pass the bootcamp, participants must earn a cumulative score of 70% or higher overall. The final exam is a 2-hour competency assessment designed to evaluate your mastery of the course material. The competency exam will directly follow bootcamp. Self-Study: DataCamp Before the required MSBA Summer Boot Camp, you may choose to complete optional, self-paced DataCamp courses for additional programming practice. These courses are not required but can help you feel more confident going into boot camp and starting your MSBA coursework. Please keep in mind you will need some datacamp modules for other courses. For instance, you need to show completion of the Python Fundamental course for CTAI, your first course in the program. Min Zhao MSBA '22 Read More from Min Zhao Residential MSBA FAQs Topics Expand All Expand Why pursue an MSBA at William & Mary? Since 1693, William & Mary has built a legacy of cultivating principled, ethical, and innovative leaders. Our Master of Science in Business Analytics (MSBA) program builds on that legacy by preparing professionals who not only understand data and technology, but also know how to use them to drive meaningful business impact. What sets William & Mary apart is the way analytics is taught—in context, through collaboration, and with purpose. Students learn to connect technical expertise with strategic insight, applying what they learn in real business settings through faculty-led projects, board engagement, and a client-based capstone. With AI and machine learning integrated throughout the curriculum, graduates leave ready to lead with data, think critically about emerging technologies, and make informed, ethical decisions in a rapidly evolving marketplace. What are some unique attributes of your program? In just 10 months, William & Mary's MSBA program immerses you in the full spectrum of modern analytics—from data management and visualization to machine learning, artificial intelligence, and cloud-based tools. Our focused curriculum, led by expert faculty, blends technical depth with strategic business application. You'll work directly with industry partners and executive mentors on real-world projects, including a client-based capstone that mirrors professional consulting experience. The result is a hands-on, AI-infused education that equips you with the skills, confidence, and perspective today's employers demand. What sort of career placement help does William & Mary provide? The Graduate Career Management Center offers personalized, dedicated career guidance to our job-seeking students. Seasoned career counselors work with our students to refine their resumes, hone interview skills in mock interviews, and develop their networks. The school hosts and participates in networking events and job fairs throughout the year, including Meet the Firms, Tech Day, and Grace Hopper, which provides our students vital connections to Fortune 500 companies and esteemed alumni looking to hire William & Mary graduates. Do all prerequisites need to be completed prior to applying? Prerequisites are not required to be completed at the time you submit your application to our program. However, they must be completed prior to the fall program start date. You can also connect with our team to find out more about transferring undergraduate or graduate credits to fulfill a prerequisite. What are some ways prerequisites can be completed? All applicants must have completed an undergraduate course in Statistics, which serves as the program's primary prerequisite. After admission, students build on that foundation through the required Tools for Analytics Bootcamp, a two week, in-person experience held in August that integrates programming and quantitative methods in R and Python. Students who wish to prepare early may complete optional DataCamp modules—such as Python Fundamentals —to strengthen their skills before bootcamp begins. Together, these experiences ensure every student enters the MSBA program confident and ready to engage with advanced analytics and AI coursework. How does the application process work? First, apply by the deadline(s). Then we holistically evaluate each applicant taking into account your academic career, GPA, quantitative background experience, personal attributes expressed in your resume, and your interview. Are there scholarship opportunities? Admitted candidates are considered for all scholarships and leadership awards if the candidate selected a need for award in their application. All scholarship support is based on merit, which includes a variety of characteristics such as professional work experience, leadership, academic history, and more. The Admissions Committee works diligently to provide financial support for as many candidates as possible through the process. Admitted students are eligible to apply for a graduate assistantship prior to the start of the program. What is the classroom like? Classes are held in Miller Hall, a beautiful, state of the art facility that reflects William & Mary's blend of tradition and innovation. The MSBA program features a dedicated classroom and collaboration space exclusively for its students, fostering a close-knit community and a highly interactive learning environment. Your cohort is divided into two sections, each with fewer than 40 students. Within each section, you'll work both independently and in small learning teams of around four, intentionally composed to bring together students from diverse academic and professional backgrounds. This structure mirrors the collaborative nature of modern analytics teams and ensures you learn as much from your peers as from your professors. What is student life like? As a William & Mary student, you become part of our family, our Tribe. Our students seamlessly integrate into the greater William & Mary community through a variety of athletic, academic, and personal enrichment opportunities. Each cohort naturally becomes a tight-knit group because they live and learn closely together. Many of our students live on campus in graduate housing . This, coupled with our collaborative, team-based cohort model of learning, cultivates close, supportive relationships among our students. Where do your graduates go after graduation? Our graduates are all over the world, working in analytics, consulting, as well as a variety of other dynamic industries. Some of our most recent graduates secured employment at Deloitte, IBM, Accenture, and KPMG. What kind of salary could I expect? Employers know the value of a William & Mary education, and our extensive alumni network enthusiastically supports our new graduates on their journey to land a job. On average, our graduates earn $85,000 annually but can earn as high as six figures in major markets. Residential MSBA Admissions Events Loading Loading... Learn more Loading... Learn more Loading...
