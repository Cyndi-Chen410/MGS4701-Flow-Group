Edwards scholarship for Women in Data Intensive Science The MPhil in Data Intensive Science is very excited to announce the creation of the Murray Edwards scholarship for Women in Data Intensive Science . This scholarship will provide full funding to study the MPhil in Data Intensive Science, with one award available each year. To be eligible, candidates must satisfy the following criteria: Be home fees status Agree, and be eligible, to join Murray Edwards college In addition to these requirements, preference will be given to candidates who have a keen interest in astronomy. Candidates who wish to communicate how they meet these preferred criteria, should use the “statement of interest” and “career goals” sections of the application form to do so. Candidates who wish to be considered for this award should apply to the MPhil and express their wish to be considered for funding before the funding deadline of the 2 nd of December. All eligible candidates who apply before the deadline will be considered. Candidates should note that they do not need to have nominated Murray Edwards as one of their preferred colleges on their application to be eligible. Interviews will be held in January and candidates will be notified shortly after. Google DeepMind AI Masters Scholarships We are also an eligible MPhil programme for the Google DeepMind AI Masters Scholarships which are administered via the Martingale Postgraduate Foundation must be applied for separately. This can be done in advance of applying to the MPhil programme as there is no requirement to have been accepted when the awards are made (as they provide application support). ACM SIGHPC Computational & Data Science Fellowships We are also happy to nominate candidates for other external funding competitions, like the ACM SIGHPC Computational & Data Science Fellowships which are targeted at women or students from racial/ethnic backgrounds that have not traditionally participated in the computing field. If you would like to be considered for nomination for this award or other similar programmes, please indicate this on your application form. There is funding for scholarships managed by the Department of Physics and MPhil participating Departments; applicants will be considered for these awards automatically if they enter the Postgraduate Funding Competition . Studentships funded by industrial and other partners may be available and are managed by the Centre for Data Intensive Science. Other funding opportunities offered by Colleges will have a variety of different deadlines which will be listed on their webpages. Other sources of funding may be available via alternate routes and should be investigated by the applicant. Applicants should indicate deadlines for acceptance related to funding they have applied for on their application. Open day Please watch the Youtube video from the Open Day 26th Oct 2023 Minimum Requirements In order to be considered for the MPhil applicants for this course should have achieved at least a good upper second class (good 2:1) honours degree from a UK university or an equivalent standard from an overseas university. If your degree is not from the UK, please check International Qualifications to find the equivalent in your country. The applicant’s degree should be in science or a technology discipline, and applicants are expected to demonstrate abilities at an adequate level in mathematics, especially in the domain of linear algebra, statistics and probability. Candidates should also demonstrate sufficient training to engage with the scientific content of at least 2 minor modules and enthusiasm for their subject areas. Criteria Applications which meet the minimum requirements are then judged against a wide range of criteria to determine their suitability for the programme. Successful applications demonstrate, amongst other things: Strong prior achievement in a mathematical discipline A clear interest in scientific data analysis High levels of motivation and independence Enthusiasm for hands on computation. The background of the current cohort is approximately: 50% physics, 25% mathematics (mostly from applied and statistics) and 25% “interesting other” (covering: chemistry, computational biology/neuroscience, material science, other quantitive natural sciences, engineering, and computer science). Widening Participation We are strongly committed to widening participation in this MPhil and very much encourage applications from students from all backgrounds and via all institutional pathways, including returners to learning and those applying from outside research-intensive institutions. We are also particularly keen to hear from students from all socio-economic backgrounds and would invite you to take note of the University’s funding competition for students from low-income households, here . Please apply early to meet the funding deadline, which for this course is the 2 nd of December This MPhil is also participating in the University’s contextual admissions pilot, where the widest context of an application is taken into account as part of the admissions process. Our course page in the postgraduate Course Directory tells you more about this, or have a look at the Information page for applicants here . We aim for all aspects of our programme to be inclusive, for instance ensuring that speakers and panel members at our events are representative. The 2025 cohort is approximately 45% female and contains applicants who are the first in their family to attend university, are mature students, and approximately 70% are international students from about different 25 nationalities. In 2025 we are continuing our programme to integrate mental health support proactively into the MPhil to better support students on the programme including dedicated events by Psychologists who specialise in wellbeing.
