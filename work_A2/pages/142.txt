As datasets grow in scale and complexity, the field demands people who can handle diverse data sources, build reliable models and communicate insights clearly – capabilities that have become essential across business, tech, health, finance and government. The interdisciplinary Master of Data Science will build your expertise in computer science, statistics and mathematics, and offer extensive opportunities for practical application. You’ll study within our state-of-the-art computing precinct designed for experimentation and technical development, and refine your skills through an industry internship or supervised research project. With our location in the Connect Macquarie Park Innovation District – home to a dynamic mix of tech-driven organisations such as Domain Holdings, truuth and Orbyte – you’ll make connections with organisations looking for people who can translate complex data into insight that informs decisions. ### Key features * **Build skills in emerging technologies**Develop capabilities in cloud and on-site data management, machine learning, statistical modelling and large-scale data systems. * **Learn from research that leads the field**Study content informed by the Data Horizons Research Centre, with teaching shaped by current practice in data science. * **Build teamwork through project-based learning**Develop collaboration, communication and problem-solving skills by working in teams on complex projects that mirror workplace practice. * **Join an active, connected student community**Network with peers in our computing and big data societies, and join workshops and hackathons sponsored by Microsoft, IBM and AWS. #### Top 100 in the world Data science and AI: QS World Uni Rankings by Subject 2025 #### Top 50 in the world Computer science and engineering, ARWU 2025 #### #1 in Sydney for computing Postgrad facilities, resources: QILT Student Exp Survey 2024 ### Career outcomes With a rapidly evolving job market, it’s vital that you have the right skills to evolve with it.
