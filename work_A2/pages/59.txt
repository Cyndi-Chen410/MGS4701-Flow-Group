Prepare to lead across industries. Join an analytics program for business people — no coding background required. Kogod is Best in Class for AI Kogod is shaping the future of AI-driven innovation. Gain the Skills that Help You Excel in the AI Economy You will emerge with highly sought-after expertise in advanced quantitative methods, machine learning, and natural language processing, coupled with a strong understanding of ethical AI practices and business acumen. Through rigorous coursework and applied projects, you will develop proficiency in data manipulation, predictive modeling, data visualization, and prompt engineering, leveraging tools like R, Python, SQL, and Tableau. Prepare for Any Field with Industry-Driven Paths With AI poised to transform every industry, you can customize your Business Analytics & AI degree and focus your electives on sustainability, marketing, finance, forensic accounting, or data science. Earn Your Degree Your Way With flexible options to study in-person, online, or anywhere in between on a full-time or part-time basis, you can tailor the program to fit your life, career, and family. All students, no matter the modality, complete their studies with an in-person hands-on practicum in Washington, DC, where you’ll work with real data to solve business challenges and build valuable professional connections. Fine-tune your degree and focus your electives on sustainability, marketing, finance, forensic accounting, or data science. Or, work with an advisor to build your own specialization! Purpose & Orientation Kogod MS in Business Analytics & AI Designed to combine business analytics and artificial intelligence: preparing students to apply AI/ML tools in business decision-making, strategy, operations, finance, marketing, etc. Typical Data Science MS More narrowly focused on technical, algorithmic, and statistical modeling; deeper work with large/complex/unstructured data, more focus on building models than translating them into business strategy. Curriculum & Skills Kogod MS in Business Analytics & AI Core in analytics foundations (data gathering, quantitative analysis, predictive modeling) using tools/languages like Python, R, SQL, Tableau. Electives and/or paths in finance, marketing, sustainability, forensic accounting, etc., or even your own specialization. Practicum (real data, real business challenge) required. Business domain exposure (strategy, operations, marketing etc.), with “whole of business” understanding. Typical Data Science MS Typical data science programs often go heavier on: advanced statistics, machine learning, deep learning, possibly data engineering; more projects or research with unstructured data; focus on technical optimization, algorithm development; possibly less emphasis on business context, strategy, or stakeholder communication. Flexibility & Modality Kogod MS in Business Analytics & AI Multiple delivery options: in-person, online, hybrid; full-time or part-time. Typical Data Science MS Some programs offer similar flexibility, but many “typical” data science MSs are full-time, on-campus, with less modularity toward non-technical electives. Technical Background Required Kogod MS in Business Analytics and AI Kogod’s program has no coding background required for admission into the program. Typical MS in Data Science Most data science programs expect incoming students to have solid background in programming (Python, R or similar), mathematics/statistics, and sometimes data structures. Outcome and Job Roles Kogod MS in Business Analytics & AI Graduates take roles such as AI Product Manager, AI Transformation Manager, Intelligence Analyst, Business Analytics Consultant, Marketing Analyst, etc. Typical MS in Data Science Typical data science graduates more often go into Data Scientist, ML Engineer, Data Engineer, Research Scientist, AI Specialist roles; also roles in data modeling, algorithm development, sometimes R&D. Communication, Business Insight & Soft Skills Kogod MS in Business Analytics & AI Strong emphasis in Kogod on communicating findings, data storytelling, and translating analytics/AI insights for business stakeholders. Typical MS in Data Science Varies by program: some data science MSs also include courses on communication, visualization, ethics, domain knowledge; many focus more narrowly on technical skills, leaving “business translation” as something you pick up on the job. What to Think About for You Consider whether you want a business-focused or technical role, your comfort level with programming and math, your need for flexible program formats, your interest in hands-on projects, and the type of employers or industries you’re targeting.
