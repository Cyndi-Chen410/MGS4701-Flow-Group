Year 1 takes place in London. Year 2 takes place in Shanghai. Read more Explore our full programme portfolio MAM FAQs Who is the MAM programme aimed at? The Masters in Analytics and Management (MAM) is aimed at recent graduates who have graduated within the last two years, with a maximum of two years of postgraduate work experience. You should have a strong quantitative skill set - gained through existing work experience or your degree – and you should want to build a strong career in the analytics and management space. What kind of career does the MAM prepare me for? The MAM programme is for those seeking a career as a Business or Data Analyst in the digital, consulting, retail, consumer goods, healthcare or finance sector. Graduates with strong analytical skills are sought after by organisations across sectors looking for data translators to drive strategic decisions. What will I be studying on this Masters in Analytics and Management programme? Applied data analytics skills and world-class general management techniques. MAM is integrated, practical, and focuses on the point where business, data science and machine learning intersect. Explore our programme content page for more details. Am I eligible for admissions? See our Apply Now page or submit your CV for review. A member of our Recruitment Team will then be in touch to discuss eligibility and next steps. Do internships taken as part of my studies count as work experience? Only work experience gained post-graduation is considered towards the two year limit requirement. Any internship taken during your studies will not count. You can, therefore, apply to the MAM having taken several internships during your studies. When should I apply? Please view the deadline information on our how to apply page . Can I visit LBS? We host talk and tours on campus , as well as a large range of online events. Please visit our events page to browse all events. You can also explore a virtual tour of the school here . Will I require a Visa? Currently, if you aren’t a national of the European Economic Area (EEA) or Switzerland, you need a visa to study in the UK. Applying for a UK visa to study at LBS is a straightforward process. For more information, please visit our visa requirements page . Do you offer any financial aid? LBS offers several financing options to support your graduate studies, including merit funds and bursaries. Around 20% of the Graduate Masters class each year receives funding from one of the School’s scholarships, which are supported by the School, alumni and external donors. In addition to our own scholarship portfolio, there are also external loan options that admitted students can apply to, including Prodigy and Future Finance.
