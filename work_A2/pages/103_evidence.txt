Master of Science in Computer Science (AI & Decentralized Technologies Stream), with Delta X Fellowship This pathway is for applicants seeking admission to the AI & Decentralized Technologies Stream , which covers artificial intelligence, Web3 and decentralized technologies. Admission through this pathway includes the Delta X Fellowship and Semester 1 study at the HKU-CDS Shanghai Teaching & Research Site. The fellowship experience includes industry immersion, networking and internship opportunities, a support package and travel subsidy subject to prevailing arrangements, and cross-border learning in Hong Kong and Shanghai. Apply HERE . 2. Master of Science in Computer Science (General, Cyber Security, Financial Computing and Multimedia Computing Streams), with Delta X Fellowship This pathway is for applicants seeking admission to one of the following streams WITH the Delta X Fellowship: General Stream Cyber Security Stream Financial Computing Stream Multimedia Computing Stream Admission through this pathway includes Semester 2 study at the HKU-CDS Shanghai Teaching & Research Site. The fellowship experience includes industry immersion, networking and internship opportunities, a support package and travel subsidy subject to prevailing arrangements, and cross-border learning in Hong Kong and Shanghai. Applicants will be invited to indicate their preferred stream during the application process. Apply HERE . 3. Master of Science in Computer Science (General, Cyber Security, Financial Computing and Multimedia Computing Streams) This pathway is for applicants seeking admission to one of the following streams WITHOUT the Delta X Fellowship: General Stream Cyber Security Stream Financial Computing Stream Multimedia Computing Stream Applicants will be invited to indicate their preferred stream during the application process. Applications submitted through this pathway will NOT be considered for the Delta X Fellowship. Apply HERE . How to Apply Review the HKU Taught Postgraduate Admissions application procedures . Select your preferred application pathway from the options listed above and complete the online application form accordingly. The selected pathway should correspond to the programme/stream you wish to apply for. Application fee: HK$780 per application (for the 2027/28 intake). When completing the online application form, please provide a valid mailing address and email address to ensure the secure and successful delivery of application-related correspondence. After submitting the online application and paying the application fee, you will receive an email notification within approximately five working days with instructions for accessing the TPG Online Admission System of the School of Computing and Data Science. Log in to the TPG Online Admission System and upload the required supporting documents. Supporting Documents Along with the application the following documents are required to be submitted: Graduate transcript(s) with grading system of all tertiary level studies issued by the Registry of institution (or interim transcript if graduate transcript is not available yet) Graduation certificate(s), if available Degree certificates (For applicant with Mainland China qualification) (i) Online Verification Report of Higher Education Qualification Certificate issued by CSSD (學信網); and (ii) Certificate of Bachelor’s Degree* (學士學位證書) issued by the Registry of awarding institution; OR Online Verification Report of Higher Education Degree Certificate (中國高等教育學位在線驗證報告) issued by CSSD (學信網) English Language proficiency test score report, such as TOEFL or IELTS (only for applicants with qualifications from institutions outside HK where the language of instruction and/or examination is not English) Proof of Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) / average grade / average mark (If the transcript does not show the cumulative GPA / average grade / average mark, or no school proof is available, please complete the online form). Official proof of class ranking, if available A curriculum vitae (C.V.) Identity documents; Visa / Entry Permit (if applicable) Applicants are NOT required to submit original documents during the application stage. Should an applicant be given an admission offer by the Programme, he/she will receive notification of the need to submit original documents. Please note that documents once submitted will not be returned, and will be destroyed if the application is unsuccessful. Admission Requirements Admission University Entrance Requirements To be eligible for admission to the programme, a candidate shall satisfy both the University Admissions Requirements including but not limited to: (a) shall comply with the General Regulations; (b) shall hold a Bachelor’s degree of this University or a qualification of equivalent standard from this University or another comparable institution accepted for this purpose; (c) for a candidate who is seeking admission on the basis of a qualification from a university or comparable institution outside Hong Kong of which the language of teaching and/or examination is not English, shall satisfy the University English language requirement applicable to high degrees as prescribed under General Regulation G2(b). (d) satisfy the examiners in a qualifying examination if required. --> Language English Language Requirement Applicants who hold a degree from a university or comparable institution outside Hong Kong where the language of teaching and/or examination was not English must satisfy the University English language requirement* for admission, unless they are granted an exceptional exemption. Fee notes Payment shall be made by two identical instalments for full-time study within the normative period of study (one year) and four identical instalments for part-time study within the normative period of study (two years). Upon acceptance of the offer of admission, applicants are required to pay a deposit consisting of the first instalment of the composition fee, the caution money (HK$350), and the student activity fee (HK$100, applicable to full-time students only). The first instalment will be applied toward the total composition fee. Fees once paid are not refundable. On leaving the University, student's caution money (HK$350) shall be used to offset the graduation fee or, if the student is not graduating, be returned to the student. Scholarship & Financial Aids The School of Computing and Data Science offers scholarships to attract and reward outstanding Master of Science in Computer Science students. Awards encompass both admission-based and merit-based support. The number and the value of each scholarship shall be determined by the Selection Committee based on the funds that are available in each year. I’m an undergraduate student in the final year. Can I apply to your programme? You can submit an application if you expect that you will complete your current study and obtain official certification on your graduation by August. Non-local students should however note that the official offer letter of admission could be issued only after you have submitted the official certificate on graduation and the complete transcript. This official offer letter is required for your student visa application which will take at least 6 to 8 weeks. Therefore, you may have to reserve enough time for visa application. 2. Can I apply to more than one pathway under the programme? Can I receive offers from multiple streams or switch to a different stream after receiving an offer? You may submit applications for more than one pathway. Applications will be reviewed independently. However, each applicant can only receive an offer to one stream. Once an offer is made, it will not be switched to or reconsidered for another stream applied. Any stream transfer request may only be considered after formal admission to the programme, subject to approval. 3. Do I need to post the hardcopy of supporting documents to HKU during application? No. Applicants are not required to post hardcopies of supporting documents during the application stage. Please upload softcopies to the TPG Online Admission System under the School of Computing and Data Science: https://tpgadmission.cds.hku.hk as soon as possible. Should an applicant be given an admission offer by the Programme, he/she will receive notification of the need to submit original documents. 4. When will I receive instructions for uploading supporting documents? After receiving the "Acknowledgement of the Receipt of Application" email, another email notification will be sent by the School of Computing and Data Science to you within 5 working days after submitting your online application, please follow the instructions in the email and upload your supporting documents in PDF format for our preliminary consideration by logging in TPg Online Admission System under the School of Computing and Data Science: https://tpgadmission.cds.hku.hk . 5. Are photocopies of the TOEFL or IELTS reports accepted? During the application stage, applicants are recommended to provide the softcopy of all supporting documents to the Taught Postgraduate (TPg) Programmes Online Admission System under the School of Computing and Data Science: https://tpgadmission.cds.hku.hk as soon as possible. Should an applicant be given an admission offer by the Programme, he/she could ask the Examination Authority to send the TOEFL / IELTS official report directly to HKU. 6. Do I need to submit a C.V. or a personal statement? Yes, you are required to submit a C.V. Personal statement is optional. 7. Do I need to submit reference letters? Is there any form for referees? You do not need to submit reference letters or ask your referees to complete a form at the time of application. The Master Programmes Office will contact you or the referees directly afterwards if reference letters are required. 8. When will I know the application results? The admission selection committee works continuously for months to review the large number of applications and to conduct interviews in order to select top candidates for admission to this MSc programme. Successful applicants will be notified of the result once it is ready. However, if you have not heard from us by 31 July 2027 , you may assume that your application is not successful. Visa Related (Non-local Applicants) 1. How to apply for the student visa? Non-local students, including those from the Mainland China, must obtain a visa in order to study in Hong Kong. Without a valid visa, they cannot register for their study in the University. The student visa will also enable students to enter Hong Kong as students. Admissions Office of the Registry can assist students in obtaining visas. Details can be found at: https://www.studentvisa.hku.hk/ . 2. I’m a non-local applicant. How do I get information about student visa, finance, accommodation and life in HKU? – Composition fees of MSc(CompSc): Composition Fees (on this page) – Support to Non-local Applicants: https://engg.hku.hk/Teaching-Learning/MSc/Student-Resources-Support (information provided by the Faculty of Engineering ) – A Glimpse of Student Life @HKU: https://www.cedars.hku.hk/nonlocal/publication/glimpse2425.pdf (information provided by the Centre of Development and Resources for Students ) 3. If I have a valid work visa or tourist visa, do I need a student visa for studying in Hong Kong? Holder of Work Visa – If you have a valid work visa for Hong Kong that covers the programme period, you will not need another visa to study in the part-time MSc Programme. Holder of Tourist Visa – Tourist visa does not allow you to study in Hong Kong. Students who do not have the right to abode in Hong Kong are required to apply for a student visa for entering into Hong Kong and studying on a full-time master degree programme.
