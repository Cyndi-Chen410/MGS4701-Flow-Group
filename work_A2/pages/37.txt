However, many applicants can be evaluated without a standardized test score. You may qualify for a GMAT/GRE waiver if you meet the criteria below. Applicants who do not qualify for a waiver must submit a valid GMAT or GRE score. _**Please note:** The MSBA Admissions Committee may require a GMAT or GRE score from any applicant if additional information is needed to evaluate academic readiness or program fit._ ### GMAT/GRE Waiver Eligibility #### UGA Terry College and Double Dawg Pathway Applicants You qualify for a waiver if you meet**at least one**of the following criteria: * Have a Data Analytics or Business Analytics Area of Emphasis * Have completed, or are currently enrolled in,**BUSN 4000**and**BUSN 5000** * Have completed, or are currently enrolled in,**BUSN 4000**and at least one**BUSN 1.5-credit programming course** * Are enrolled in one of the following majors: * Economics AB * Economics BBA * Finance BBA * Management BBA * Management Information Systems BBA * Marketing BBA * Mathematics BS * Risk Management and Insurance BBA * Statistics BS #### Other UGA Students and Applicants from Accredited U.S. Institutions You qualify for a waiver if you meet**at least one**of the following criteria: * Have an overall GPA of**3.3 or higher**and earned grades of**B or higher**in both Calculus and Statistics (or equivalent courses) * Have an overall GPA of**3.0 or higher**and**one or more years of full-time professional work experience**after earning a bachelor’s degree ### Who Must Submit a GMAT or GRE Score? A valid GMAT or GRE score is required for: * Applicants who do not meet any of the waiver criteria above * International applicants who do not qualify for a waiver based on attendance at an accredited U.S. institution Consider submitting a test score even if you qualify for a waiver: * A GMAT or GRE score can strengthen your application by providing additional evidence of academic readiness. * Some consulting and finance employers may require a GMAT or GRE score during their recruiting or hiring process. ## International Applicants International applicants must also submit[**TOEFL or IELTS**](https://grad.uga.edu/index.php/prospective-students/international-application-information/international-supplement/english-language-proficiency-requirement/)scores as required by the UGA Graduate School. Please visit the[UGA Bursar’s Office website](https://busfin.uga.edu/finance-division/bursar/students-parents/bursar-tuition-fees/)for the most current tuition and fee information. ## Admission FAQs Why do I need an internship or full-time work experience? The Admissions Committee strongly prefers candidates entering the MSBA directly from an undergraduate program to have completed at least one internship. Candidates who graduated more than a year before applying should have full-time work experience. Professional experience helps students connect classroom concepts to real-world situations and contribute more effectively to coursework and the capstone project. It also makes candidates more competitive for employment after graduation. Entering the MSBA with limited professional experience may reduce employment opportunities and ultimately impact the return on your investment in the program. [Video 13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1tf4U2KjkOc) What should I include in my résumé? Please include your employment and educational history in reverse chronological order (most recent first). For internships/work experience, include titles, month and year durations, and whether you worked part-time or full-time. Your educational record should indicate dates of attendance and degree(s) earned. Feel free to integrate additional details that quantify your accomplishments and offer context to your achievements. Whenever possible, incorporate information about extracurricular activities, community involvement, and personal interests. [Video 14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P7eIEZL3Lxw) What are the transcript requirements? The University of Georgia requires that all MSBA applicants possess a baccalaureate degree or its foreign equivalent from a regionally accredited institution. Please refer to the Graduate School website for more information about requirements for [domestic](https://grad.uga.edu/index.php/prospective-students/domestic-application-information/)and [international](https://grad.uga.edu/index.php/prospective-students/international-application-information/) students. Candidates are expected to have a minimum 3.0 GPA. While applying with a GPA below this threshold is permissible, addressing your academic performance in the application is strongly recommended. Official transcripts from all institutions attended are required only after admission. Current and former UGA students do not need to request official UGA transcripts. [Video 16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C_nM-sNWw8Y) What should I include in my short written responses? The required written responses help the Admissions Committee understand your career goals, technical preparation, and readiness for the MSBA program. Be specific and use examples from your academic, professional, internship, or project experience. **_Career Goals_** _What are your short-term career goals immediately after completing the MSBA program? How do those goals connect to your long-term professional aspirations?_ We want to understand what you hope to do with your MSBA and how the program fits into your broader career plans. Your goals may change, and that’s okay. We are looking for evidence that you have thoughtfully considered your next steps and that your goals are realistic and aligned with what the MSBA can help you achieve. The Career Management Center (CMC) provides personalized support throughout the program, including career coaching, resume reviews, interview preparation, networking opportunities, and employer connections to help students prepare for their job search and long-term career success. _**\_**Technical Experience**\_**\_Describe your experience with programming languages or technical tools, such as Python, R, SQL, Tableau, Power BI, Excel, or others. Provide one or two specific examples of how you have used these tools in coursework, projects, internships, or work.\__ You do not need to have experience with every tool listed. We want to understand your current level of technical preparation and how you have applied those skills. Specific examples help us better understand your experience and readiness for the quantitative and technical demands of the program. _**Optional Written Response**_ Use the optional written response to provide relevant context or information that is not addressed elsewhere in your application. This may include explaining circumstances that affected your academic or professional record, clarifying something in your application, or sharing other information you believe would be helpful to the Admissions Committee._**\_**Optional Video Essay**\_**_ The optional video essay gives you another opportunity to share more about yourself and your personality beyond what we see in the written application. Details and prompts are provided in the application. Will there be an interview during the application process? Interviews are by invitation only. After reviewing completed applications, the Admissions Committee may invite select candidates to interview.
