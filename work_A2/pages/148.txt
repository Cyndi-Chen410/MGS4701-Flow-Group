Study Plan Master of Science in Business Analytics Program Study Plan (Applicable for students admitted in the academic year 2020/2021 and afterward) I. Program Duration The normal study period of Master of Science in Business Analytics program is 2 years. The maximum study period is 3 years. (Students admitted in or before academic year 2019/2020, please refer to the rules and regulations as stipulated in the intake academic year study plan.) II. Study Mode Lectures III. Medium of Instruction Chinese and English IV. Academic Field Management Science and Application V. Course Structure This program includes 11 core courses and a master's degree thesis. Table I: Core Courses (24 credits) Course Code Course Title Credits MBSZ01 Applied Statistics 3 MBSZ02 Enterprise Information Management 3 MBSZ03 Managerial Economics 3 MBSZ04 Data Mining 3 MBSZ05 Research Methodology 3 MBSZ06 Game Theory 3 MBSZ07 Business Analytics with Practice 3 MBSZ08 Operations Analysis 3 Total credits 24 Table II: Core Courses* (9 credits) Course Code Course Title Credits A. Select one core course from the following two courses. MBSZ09 Contemporary Topics in Supply Chain Management 3 MBSZ10 Special Topic in Supply Chain Finance 3 B. Select one core course from the following two courses. MBSZ11 Big Data Analytics 3 MBSZ12 R Application in Quantitative Analysis 3 C. Select one core course from the following two courses. MBSZ13 Information Technology Service Management 3 MBSZ14 Electronic Commerce and Application 3 Total credits 9 * The University has the ultimate right to decide which course or whether the course will be opened for all core subjects in table II. Table III: Thesis (12 credits) Course Code Course Title Credits MBSZ15 Thesis 12 VI. Teaching Requirements Students are required to complete 33 credits, including 11 core courses regulated by study plan, during study period. Students have to complete a research thesis which is not less than 20,000 words and pass the thesis oral defense. After finishing the course work, students will be assessed by different ways depending on regulations established by the instructor and MSB. VII. Study Time The duration for taking all courses is 18 months and the duration for thesis writing is 6 months. In general, classes will be arranged from Monday to Friday daytime or night. (Classes will be scheduled during daytime in the Academic Year 2023/2024) VIII. Graduation Requirements Upon approval from the Senate of the University, a Master’s Degree will be conferred on a student when he or she has: Completed and met the requirements prescribed in the study plan of his or her program within the specific study period, and achieved a cumulative GPA of 2.50 or above (excluding thesis) Abided by the regulations of the University Cleared all fees and charges and returned all University’s property and equipment borrowed If students pass all courses required in Table I and II above with a cumulative GPA of 2.5 or above, but fail to submit or pass the final thesis oral defense during the specified period, he/she can only get a completion certificate.
