Applicants are evaluated based on their academic performance and leadership potential, as demonstrated through their submitted application materials. Résumé Two essays (one video and one written) Unofficial transcripts from all institutions attended, with English translation if needed. Two references (academic or professional) Official TOEFL or IELTS score (International students, if applicable) GMAT or GRE is not required. Candidates are encouraged to showcase quantitative ability through coursework or professional experience to handle the rigors of the program. While test scores provide an additional, relevant data point for the admissions committee to consider, a test score is optional. $150 application fee. Application fee waivers are available for attending select recruitment events. Application fees paid prior to waiver approval are non-refundable. It is your responsibility to ensure that all components of your application are complete by the deadline for timely review. Incomplete applications will automatically be considered in the next round. Once your completed application is submitted, you can expect a decision within two months. All applicants are automatically considered for scholarships. Submitting your application early generally improves your chances of receiving a scholarship if you qualify.
