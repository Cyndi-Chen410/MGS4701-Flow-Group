Consultation Why Earn a Master of Science in Business Analytics & AI at the Smith School? Develop Analytical Skills That Drive Better Business Decisions The Robert H. Smith School of Business prepares you to become the analytical problem-solver organizations need to tackle today's complex business challenges. In the Master of Science in Business Analytics & AI program, you'll learn to transform data into actionable insights using statistical modeling, data visualization, predictive analytics, responsible AI and business intelligence tools. Through experiential learning projects and guidance from expert faculty, you'll apply analytics and AI to solve real business problems, lead data-driven decision-making and create meaningful impact. 30 Credits 3 Semesters #12 Master’s in Business Analytics (US Public) QS, 2027 View previous stat View next stat Why Smith? Develop data analytics and AI expertise through a comprehensive graduate curriculum. Apply what you learn through experiential learning projects using real-world business data Learn from faculty whose research is advancing data analytics. Collaborate with peers from diverse academic backgrounds through team-based projects. Who Is the Master of Science in Business Analytics & AI For? Data-Driven Problem-Solvers The Master of Science in Business Analytics & AI is designed for recent graduates from a diverse set of backgrounds who have an interest in using data to tell a story and solve complex business problems. You'll build the technical and strategic skills needed to apply AI responsibly, uncover meaningful insights from data and help organizations make informed decisions. No prior coding or technical experience is required. You'll develop your quantitative skills throughout the program, including optional bridge coursework before classes begin. Ideal candidates include: Analytical thinkers who want to solve business problems with data. Recent graduates seeking specialized expertise in data analytics and AI. Student-athletes interested in careers in sports analytics. Computer science graduates seeking applied business experience. No matter your professional or academic background, you'll graduate with proficiency in AI and data analytics, the ability to generate data-driven business insights and communication skills to explain complex findings to others. What Will You Learn in the Master of Science in Business Analytics & AI Program? Develop the Skills Driving the Future of Business The Master of Science in Business Analytics & AI curriculum emphasizes critical thinking, preparing you for roles that continue to experience strong market demand. Through coursework and experiential projects, you'll learn the best ways to communicate your insights to influence organizational decision-making. Artificial Intelligence Analyze large datasets more efficiently, identify patterns and trends, and support business decisions with responsible AI. Data-Driven Decision-Making Use data to evaluate business challenges, assess opportunities and develop strategic recommendations. Data Visualization Create dashboards and visualizations that communicate key metrics, monitor performance, and support informed decision-making. Coding Learn programming languages like Python, R and SQL to collect, organize, prepare and analyze data. Decision Analytics Evaluate business scenarios, assess risk and recommend data-informed strategies. Simulation and Optimization Develop models that simulate business scenarios, evaluate potential outcomes and optimize decisions for improvement. Apply What You Learn You’ll develop these skills through: Experiential learning Machine learning projects Business case studies Portfolio-building projects By graduation, you’ll be proficient in using AI and analytical tools to make informed decisions and solve complex business challenges. Frequently Asked Questions Is a Master of Science in Business Analytics & AI worth it? Yes. Organizations increasingly rely on data and AI to inform strategic decisions. This degree prepares you with the analytical, technical and business skills needed to create value across industries while positioning you for strong career outcomes in one of today's fastest-growing fields. Do I need a degree in business to apply? No. The Master of Science in Business Analytics & AI welcomes students from a diverse set of academic backgrounds, including communications, sports science and computer science. Successful applicants bring transferable skills such as critical thinking, strategic analysis and problem-solving. We’ll prepare you to develop the technical expertise needed to thrive in business analytics careers. What makes Smith’s Master of Science in Business Analytics & AI different? The Master of Science in Business Analytics & AI combines technical analytics skills with business strategy, responsible AI and experiential learning. You'll gain hands-on experience using AI and analytics tools while learning to communicate insights, make data-driven decisions and solve complex business challenges. Have More Questions?
