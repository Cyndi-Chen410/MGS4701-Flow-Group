2028 The Admissions Committee is now accepting applications for the MSBAi Class of 2028, which begins in May of 2027. The application deadline information is below, and will be updated throughout the recruitment cycle: Early Action Deadline: July 27th, 2026 ▷ Apply early to receive an expedited review of your application Regular Decision Deadlines ▷ Apply to receive an admissions decision in 3-4 weeks Regular Decision Deadline I: October 30th, 2026 Regular Decision Deadline II: January 15th, 2027 Final Deadline: March 1st, 2027 ▷ Apply before this date to be considered for the MSBAi Class of 2028 Applications will be reviewed on a rolling basis, and a decision will be made within 3-4 weeks after the application becomes complete. For a review of your candidacy and the option for an informational call, please complete our preliminary information form. Take the first step to secure your place in the Class of 2028! Please note there may be a delay in our communication due to the application turnover period. Admissions Criteria The MS in Business Analytics and AI program is designed for experienced professionals who help drive strategy through analyzing data. Therefore, a candidate's record of professional achievement is a critical factor. To apply to the MSBAi program, you must have: A completed bachelor's degree (or an equivalent degree) with a strong academic record/Grade Point Average Full-time, professional, work experience gained after completing the required undergraduate degree There is no minimum number of years of work experience required Internships and trainee positions do not qualify Demonstrated academic preparedness, evidenced through prior coursework, professional experience, and/or relevant certifications Admissions Process The Admissions Process is rigorous and selective. We consider an applicant's candidacy holistically based on the qualities they will bring to the MSBAi program. To begin: STEP ONE Submit your resume with the preliminary information form , which will assist the Admissions Committee in advising you on your suitability for the program. The Admissions Office will contact you within one week to confirm receipt and to follow up on any questions you may have about the program or application process. STEP TWO Complete the online application . ​As part of the online application, we will need the following: One essay (topic provided on application platform) Resume One recommendation from a current or most recent supervisor (to be submitted via an online form on the application system) Official transcripts in English Official transcripts must be submitted to the Admissions Committee and sent directly from the university where you received your degree Non-refundable application fee of $103 GMAT and GRE not required for this program Please note that you may submit your application for initial review even if recommendations or transcripts are still being processed or are not yet ready to be submitted. Prior to starting the program, enrolled students will be required to acknowledge that they have communicated the time commitment the program requires to their employers. For tips on filling out your application, view our series of application tip videos on the Resources and Videos page ! STEP THREE (Invitation Only) Admissions Interview. Should the Admissions Committee feel positive about your candidacy upon review of a completed application, we will contact you to schedule an admissions interview.
