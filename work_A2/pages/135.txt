Read transcript close Close transcript Where could a Master of Data Science and Innovation take you? transcript I had reached a standstill in my career as a software engineer, and I was ready for something more: to solve complex problems and shape what comes next. As a woman in data science, I didn't just want to enter the field. I wanted to help lead it. That's when I discovered the Master of Data Science and Innovation at UTS. What struck me wasn't just the program, but the stories I heard from alumni — stories of transformation, of getting hands-on to drive meaningful change. One of the things that really set the program apart was iLab. It's what makes the degree so unique. In iLab, you work closely with industry partners on real data challenges they're facing. It felt like an internship built into the course — a chance to apply everything we were learning in real time with tangible impact. The atmosphere at UTS is incredibly collaborative. It feels like everyone's voice matters. The facilities are designed to bring people together. I always feel encouraged to step up, share ideas, and even present on larger stages. One of the biggest surprises was how much I grew beyond technical skills. I developed soft skills like project and people management, and those have genuinely shaped who I am today. Being surrounded by other women in the program was incredibly empowering. We shared ideas, supported each other, and pushed boundaries together. They inspired me to keep challenging myself and discover what I'm truly capable of. The support from lecturers and tutors was really special. They genuinely cared about my success, and by the end of the programme, they felt more like coworkers who were invested in my growth. The program didn't just prepare me for the future job market. It gave me the tools to make an impact and the confidence to know that I can. Admissions information Admissions information Use the buttons to expand or collapse the section. Course description This course equips you with the essential knowledge and skills to tackle data science tasks for real-world business problems. You will perform descriptive, predictive, and prescriptive analytics from ethical and human-centered perspectives while exploring innovations in the field of data science. Choose this program for its unique transdisciplinary approach, blending diverse fields with practical industry experience. Stand out with skills in transforming practices through analytics. Gain expertise in data analysis, project management, and strategic implementation, all tailored to current market demands. Learn through a mix of online and in-person sessions at UTS's advanced facilities, enriched by industry partnerships. Engage with real-world data projects, ensuring your learning is relevant and applied. The surge in data across industries fuels a global demand for your future skills. The dramatic growth of data in every conceivable industry, from oceanography to market research, presents another major driving force in generating unprecedented global demand for data science skills. Information for Masters by Research students: Government-funded scholarship places available As a recipient of the Australian Government’s Research Training Program (RTP) Fees Offset Scholarship, you do not have to pay tuition fees for up to the maximum period of candidature. However, even with this scholarship, you should expect ongoing costs that aren’t covered, like travel, training, and professional development. These costs can vary for each person, so it’s important to plan accordingly. Additionally, scholarships are available to help with your living expenses. Important details: The RTP Fees Offset Scholarship covers your tuition fees, but please note that it doesn’t include additional costs related to studying or conducting research. You can find the inherent requirements for your course via the inherent requirements directory . Recognition of prior learning A maximum of 48cp of Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) may be granted for the course. This may consist of a maximum of 24cp unspecified credit being granted based on relevant undergraduate studies, all other RPL must be on the basis of prior postgraduate study at an Australian university, or at a recognised overseas institution deemed to be equivalent to an Australian university. To be eligible for recognition of prior learning, the subject being considered for prior study must have been completed within five years of commencing the course. Recognition of study completed before this period is not considered. Important notes Depending on qualifications and work experience, C04372 can be taken with either 2-year duration or 1.5-year duration. If you have relevant work experience, you may qualify for the accelerated version of the course with 1-year full time duration C04370 .
