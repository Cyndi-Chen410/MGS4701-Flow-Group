Full-time Admissions Admissions - Full-time Master of Business Analytics Full-time Master of Business Analytics Admissions Main navigation Curriculum Admissions Application Process Connect with a Student Events International Admissions Tuition & Aid Careers Case Competition Contact Us Getting in, getting started We enroll new students in the fall semester only, which begins in late August every year. Admission is competitive and there is limited capacity, so we recommend applying early. We make decisions on a rolling basis, meaning that we consider each application as it's completed rather than considering in large batches. Apply Now Events Request Info Application deadlines Admission to the program is extremely competitive, and our cohorts typically fill prior to the final application deadline. We strongly encourage you to apply by the December 15 priority deadline. Fall (August) Priority deadline for merit-based awards: December 15 Final international deadline: March 15 Final domestic deadline: June 15 Applications open August 15 Application fees Domestic applicants: $60 International applicants: $100 Minimum requirements You must meet all of the below requirements to be considered for admission. GMAT/GRE scores are optional. Bachelor’s degree Earned from a regionally accredited college/university or equivalent from another country. We accept students from all undergraduate majors; a business degree is not required. GPA Cumulative 3.0 or better on a 4.0 scale, or foreign equivalent. English proficiency: TOEFL/IELTS/Duolingo Minimum TOEFL iBT score 100 (taken before Jan 2026) or 5.0 (taken after Jan 2026), 7.0 IELTS with no sub-score below 6.0, and Duolingo 120+. Waivers available for those who qualify. Accelerated master’s program: Fast-track your master’s degree Tippie undergraduates interested in a master's in business analytics can start taking graduate courses during their junior year. This gives you the opportunity to cross-count coursework toward your graduate program without making your undergraduate course load any heavier. Talk to your advisor about creating plan of study that supports your journey.
