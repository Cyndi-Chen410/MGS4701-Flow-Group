Learning Outcomes for the Master's in Data Science When you graduate from Boston University's Master's in Data Science program, you won't just know data science—you'll own it. Design and Master Models Gain mastery of the principal tools for data decision-making, including defining models, learning model parameters, and making accurate predictions. Wrangle Massive Datasets Achieve expertise in the management and analysis of massive, complex datasets across any industry. Drive Applied Solutions Demonstrate expertise in applying data science tools to address substantive questions in specialized areas, using sophisticated methods tailored for each domain. Build the Future Stack Extend the core tools of data decision-making by building specialized computational pipelines, automating data workflows, and developing intuitive human-computer interfaces. Translate and Explain Results Master the ability to interpret and effectively explain complex results, including assessing uncertainty and developing clear, compelling data visualizations. Lead with Ethics and Policy You will gain critical awareness of the social impacts of data-centered methods, ensuring you understand and adhere to ethical norms, fairness, privacy, and security policy. Your Academic Background You’ll need a bachelor’s or master’s in a field connected to data-driven work. Strong fits include core data-science areas like Computer Science , Electrical & Computer Engineering , or Mathematics & Statistics —with experience in algorithms, systems, programming, and theory. Coming from an Applied Field? We also welcome degrees in areas that use data science, such as Information Systems , Economics , Bioinformatics , Physics , Astronomy , Earth & Environment , or Emerging Media (and related fields). If that’s you, please make sure you meet the prerequisites listed below. Prerequisites We recommend that prospective applicants have completed 4 or more of the courses equivalent to the list below prior to applying to the program. Introductory Programming course (eg, Python, Java, C++, R) equivalent to CDS DS 110 Second course in Programming, equivalent to CDS DS 210 (Programming for Data Science) Introductory Discrete Mathematics Linear Algebra Introductory Statistics and Probability Calculus, to the level of Calculus BC Advanced Placement Introduction to Algorithms Missing a Prerequisite? If your background is strong but you’re short on one or two requirements, you may be admitted with prerequisite courses to get you up to speed.
