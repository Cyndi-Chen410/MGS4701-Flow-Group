Apply Now Request More Information Program Snapshot Master of Science 100% online Part-time 30 credits Finish in 1-2 years GRE not required Offered through the Rabb School of Continuing Studies Turn data into evidence-based decisions Organizations have more data than ever, and the need now is for professionals who can apply AI and data science to build analytics systems that improve operations and drive decisions. In Brandeis Online’s MS in Applied Data Science and Decision Analytics, you'll learn to model complex problems, deploy scalable systems, and communicate insights in ways that move organizations forward. As a student in the applied data science and decision analytics graduate program, you learn to: Apply statistical modeling, causal reasoning and experimental design. Develop machine learning and AI-powered systems. Build data engineering workflows and cloud-based data pipelines. Support machine learning operations and production deployment. Apply data governance, ethics and responsible AI frameworks. Translate analytical results into strategic, decision-oriented insights. While most data science programs focus narrowly on engineering or theory, this program integrates responsible AI, governance frameworks and sustainable data infrastructure throughout the curriculum in order to prepare you to lead in organizations where ethical and operational rigor both matter. Why earn an Online Master’s in Applied Data Science and Decision Analytics at Brandeis Online? Whether you're a business analyst ready to move into data leadership, an operations manager looking to build AI fluency, or a governance professional expanding into responsible AI, Brandeis Online’s online data science program meets you where you are. While many programs focus narrowly on theory or engineering, we prepare interdisciplinary practitioners who can bridge analytics, operations, governance, and strategy. The curriculum is designed for working professionals: asynchronous, fully online, and built around real-world analytics challenges rather than textbook theory. You benefit from: A flexible, fully online format designed for professionals balancing coursework with work and personal responsibilities. Asynchronous courses that allow you to complete work from anywhere. Small, interactive classes that allow for individual attention and meaningful faculty engagement. Faculty and advisory council members who bring applied data science, analytics and AI expertise into the learning experience. A curriculum that emphasizes responsible and ethical AI use throughout the program. Applied training in AI-ready data, governance frameworks and sustainable infrastructure. Project-based coursework using real-world datasets and practical analytics challenges. What can you do with an Applied Data Science and Decision Analytics Degree? Brandeis Online’s applied data science and decision analytics degree equips you with the technical and strategic skills needed to use data in healthcare, finance, technology, research, government and other settings. As a graduate, you can help organizations in every industry make data-driven, evidence-based decisions. The degree supports work in areas such as: Applied data science Decision analytics Machine learning engineering Data engineering and cloud analytics Business intelligence and visual analytics MLOps and production analytics Responsible AI and data governance Forecasting and operational analytics Program evaluation and impact assessment Analytics strategy and leadership This program is designed for professionals who want to build trustworthy, scalable analytics systems and communicate technical insights clearly to organizational stakeholders. Program requirements Interested in earning your MS in Applied Data Science and Decision Analytics with Brandeis Online? Learn more about program prerequisites and required courses. Expand All Prerequisites In addition to general admissions requirements, applicants to the MS in Applied Data Science and Decision Analytics should meet the following criteria: Bachelor’s degree from an accredited institution in computer science, statistics, engineering, economics, public health or a related field. Applicants with other undergraduate degree backgrounds and strong technical experience may also be considered. Applicants typically bring one to three years of professional experience, though strong candidates from a range of backgrounds are welcome. Recent graduates and career changers without programming experience can begin with RADS 100: Foundations of Applied Data Science and Decision Analytics—available as your first course in the program, and a supported on-ramp for students new to Python or statistics. Applicants without programming and statistical experience must take Foundations of Applied Data Science and Decision Analytics as their first course. FIND COURSE Prerequisites Courses The curriculum puts responsible and sustainable technology front and center, recognizing that modern data systems must be not only accurate and scalable, but also transparent, energy-conscious, and socially responsible. Required Courses RADS 100 — Foundations of Applied Data Science and Decision Analytics RADS 105 — Statistical Modeling and Causal Inference RADS 110 — Machine Learning Engineering and MLOps RADS 115 — Data Engineering and Cloud Pipelines RADS 120 — Experimental Design and Program Evaluation RADS 125 — Responsible and Sustainable AI, Ethics, and Governance RADS 130 — Business Intelligence and Visual Analytics RADS 135 — Time Series Forecasting and Operational Analytics RADS 190 — Capstone: Applied Analytics Project Electives (choose one) RADS 140 — Generative AI and Large Language Models in Analytics RADS 145 — Data Strategy and Analytics Leadership Already enrolled?
