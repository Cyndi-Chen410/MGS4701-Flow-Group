Markdown Content: The Master of Science in Data Science and Machine Learning (DSML) is an interdisciplinary graduate degree programme aimed at developing future leaders in data science and artificial intelligence. This programme is offered by the Faculty of Science’s Department of Mathematics, in collaboration with the Department of Statistics and Data Science and the School of Computing’s Department of Computer Science. Supported by leading researchers from NUS in the fields of data science and artificial intelligence, along with industry data scientists, the curriculum of this programme offers a comprehensive interdisciplinary education. The curriculum encompasses fundamental disciplines such as computer science, mathematics and statistics, along with specialised areas like machine learning and artificial intelligence.
