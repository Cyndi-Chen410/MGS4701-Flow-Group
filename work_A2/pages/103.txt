Fellowship Schemes 01 Overview 02 AI x Web3 --> 02 Delta X MSc CompSc AI x Web3 Cyber Security Multimedia Computing Financial Computing General Master of Science in Computer Science Programme Overview The MSc(CompSc) programme is a comprehensive postgraduate study programme provides advanced and in-depth learning in Computer Science. The programme is credit unit based, with courses of both applied and fundamental nature. Students can choose focused streams of study or a comprehensive and diversified study in Computer Science, i.e. the General Stream. Focused streams of study represent a cohesive collection of related courses that provides both fundamental and in-depth exposure to students in a given ICT domain. Students can extend and deepen their knowledge in the specialization streams in AI & Decentralized Technologies (AI x Web3), Cyber Security, Multimedia Computing and Financial Computing. Apply Now Streams of Study 01 AI & Decentralized Technologies New Covers machine learning, blockchain, DeFi, privacy, security, and ethics, preparing graduates for modern decentralized digital systems. Delta X Fellowship Included 02 Cyber Security Applies advanced cyber defence technologies, examining threats and protection from technical, organisational, human, and legal perspectives. Delta X Fellowship – Merit Award 03 Multimedia Computing Covers media coding, processing, real-time protocols, streaming middleware, representations, media analysis, interaction, and visualization methods. Delta X Fellowship – Merit Award 04 Financial Computing Emphasises practical numerical methods and focuses on techniques that are directly applicable to business and financial analysis. Delta X Fellowship – Merit Award 05 General Stream Offers students the most flexibility to design their own study portfolio based on their career aspiration or requirements. Delta X Fellowship – Merit Award Delta X Tech Immersion Fellowship Available to taught postrgraduate students admitted in 2026/27. This fellowship offers a unique immersion experience at the HKU-CDS Shanghai Site in Zhangjiang Hi-Tech Park. Having the flexibility to design my own studies and select interesting courses as well as having the opportunity to learn from top professors really appealed to me. The coursework was challenging yet fulfilling, and topics were relevant for today's fast-changing world. I have been able to work with cutting-edge robotic systems during my dissertation project, and I am excited to begin a new career in the technology field armed with my MSc from HKU. First, MSc(CompSc) offers a range of courses tailored to meet the specific academic and professional goals of each student. It was an incredible experience that I don't think I could have anywhere else. Mr. Hu Tianyi 2016 Student “ In the past year, I benefit a lot from tough courses in this program, which makes up my deficiency in coding experience and brings me edge-cutting knowledge.
